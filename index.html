What it never saves

  • Anything copied in Incognito
  • Password and card fields
  • Banking, login and checkout pages
  • Private keys, JWTs and API tokens

Why it asks for permissions

  • All sites — to see what you copy on the page
  • Clipboard — to copy a saved clip back to you
  • Storage — to keep your data on this device
  • Side panel — to show Memex next to the page

You’re in control

  • Off by default, pause any time
  • No account, no sign-in, nothing to sync
  • Delete any clip, or remove the extension to erase everything

Questions,
answered.

Does Memex send my data anywhere?

No. Everything is stored with your browser’s local extension storage on your device. Memex has no account system, no analytics and no server to send anything to. Our privacy policy spells it out.

Will it save my passwords?

No. Memex skips password, one-time-code and card fields, banking and login pages, Incognito windows, and anything that looks like a private key, JWT or API token.

How big can a copy be?

Up to 2,000,000 characters per clip. In the panel a long clip shows a short preview with its size and line count, and “Show full clip” expands it. Copy always returns the complete text.

It didn’t capture something I copied.

Check that Clipboard is switched on in the side panel. Memex can’t see copies made in the address bar or on chrome:// pages. For local files, enable “Allow access to file URLs” on the extension’s details page and reload the tab.

How do I open it?

Click the Memex icon, or use Ctrl Shift C for clips, Ctrl Shift N for a new note and Ctrl Shift F for search. On Mac, use ⌘. You can change them at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Is Memex free?

Yes. There’s nothing to pay for and nothing to sign up for.
