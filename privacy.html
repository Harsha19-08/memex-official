Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Memex is built to be private by design. In short: everything stays on your device, and Memex sends nothing to us or to anyone else.

What Memex stores

  • Clips — text you copy while clipboard memory is switched on, plus the site it came from and when.
  • Notes — notes you write, or save from a page with “Save to Memex Notes”, including the page title and address.
  • Todos and your settings (theme, whether clipboard memory is on).

All of this is kept in your browser’s local extension storage on your computer. It never leaves it. We have no servers, no accounts and no analytics, so we cannot see your data.

What Memex does not save

  • Anything copied in Incognito windows.
  • Text copied from password, one-time-code and payment-card fields.
  • Copies made on banking, sign-in, password-reset and checkout pages.
  • Text that looks like a private key, JSON Web Token, bearer token or common API key.

Why Memex asks for permissions

Access to all sitesA small script runs on pages so Memex can see when you copy text there. The text is only handed to the extension’s own local storage.
Clipboard read / writeTo copy a saved clip or note back to your clipboard, and to capture the clipboard when you press Capture.
Storage / unlimited storageTo keep your clips, notes and todos on your device, including very large copies.
Side panelTo show Memex next to the page you’re on.
Context menusTo add “Save to Memex Notes” when you right-click selected text.
ScriptingTo read the full text of your selection when you save it as a note, so long selections aren’t cut short.

Sharing and selling

We don’t collect your data, so there is nothing to share, sell or use for advertising. Memex does not use remote code.

Deleting your data

Delete any clip, note or todo inside the side panel. Removing the extension from your browser erases everything Memex has stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above.
